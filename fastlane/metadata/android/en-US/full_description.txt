Swipe typing done right. Highly customizable, no tracking, no AI claptrap.

Curmudgeon is fed up with silly old one-size-fits-all keyboards, keyboards that couldn't swipe correctly if their life depended on it.

Curmudgeon understands that your keyboard should do exactly what you want it to, no more and no less. It wants you to swipe blazingly fast without even looking down at the keyboard. It knows it can get things wrong, so it gives you dozens of suggestions. It doesn't second-guess you, and it prioritizes your words even if it doesn't understand them. And if you still want more, you can go under the hood and tinker with the swiping algorithm.

You can customize everything: swiping behavior, how many suggestions and what shows where, layouts and fonts, undo/redo lengths, clipboard memory.
Oh, and pressing space after swiping is no longer mandatory.

You're the boss!
