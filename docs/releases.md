# Curmudgeon Keyboard releases

Version names are `0.MINOR.NNN`, version codes `MINOR * 1000 + NNN` (0.1.0 went out as code 1). One release per settings
area; the build files are kept as `Curmudgeon_Keyboard_<name>_vc<code>` next to each other.

| Release | Code | Theme | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1.0 | 1 ("1000") | Launch MVP | First Play closed-test build: our own swipe decoder, Midnight look, symbol popup map, simple / advanced settings. |
| 0.1.001 | 1001 | Test releasing | The Play release path end to end: own signing key, internal-testing release. |
| 0.1.002 | 1002 | Refine preferences | Keyboards with their own languages, layouts and (optionally) settings; the Preferences screen rebuilt around input and layout; popups per key, spiral duplicate removal; clipboard history by length. |
| 0.1.003 | 1003 | Refine appearance | Appearance with a live preview keyboard, accept / reject, saved and built-in themes; text style dialogs (font list, size, bold / italic / underline); hide-symbols switch; key sound choice and phone-setting hints for sound and vibration. |
| 0.1.004 | 1004 | Optimize code | Clean-up: repeated code merged, dead code removed, less work per swipe and per suggestion. |
| 0.2.000 | 2000 | Own swipe decoder, rebuilt | Everything in 0.1.004 so far, plus a swipe decoder built only on long-published methods: Swype-style word picking (first and last letter where the swipe starts and ends, letters matched in order along the path, a wider second search if nothing fits); inflection points scored together with where the whole path runs; slowing down over a key as a soft preference; stops no longer mark letters. The swiped word shows in the suggestion strip only. Replay of 469 recorded swipes: top-3 85.1 %, top-8 92.5 %. |
| 0.2.001 | 2001 | Swipe results and previews (not released on its own) | A word typed up to its apostrophe suggests its contractions first. The swipe recorder captures a swiped word edited into another; an opt-in log of every swipe's result (first choice right, picked from the strip, never offered) with the phone's decode time, also shown in the swipe statistics. More Layout & Typing rows try themselves on the preview keyboard. No decoder changes. |
| 0.3.000 | 3000 | Swipe engine, measured | Everything in 0.2.001, plus the swipe decoder changes measured on 1,428 of our own swipes and 40,245 FUTO swipes: a corner after a long reach may stop short of its key; fast swipes lean more on word frequency and get relaxed corner matching. First choice 68.8 → 71.3 % and 80.2 → 82.1 %; words never offered 11.8 → 9.9 % and 8.3 → 7.2 %; decode time unchanged (11 ms on the P11). |
| 0.3.001 | 3001 | Fixes to 0.3.000 | The swipe results log actually logs (it wrote nothing unless the corpus recorder was on too); fast swipes no longer keep words that must be rejected; auto-capitalised swipes are not counted as edits; a swiped word re-opened and corrected from the strip counts as picked; "Clear the log" clears at once. Holding backspace deletes whole words even in fields that leave the cursor position unknown (a chat app's composer), on every tick. |
| 0.3.002 | 3002 | Fixes and settings rounds | The keyboard loads again with suggestions off (3000 / 3001 failed there); per-keyboard background pictures with a shared picture list; the emoji font in the font list; swipe and text-correction settings rounds. |
| 0.3.003 | 3003 | Layouts, themes, trust | Saved Layouts (everything on Layout & Typing under a name, popups included) and Keys & Popups; themes include the emoji look; Midnight / Daylight default look with a light / dark pairing; colour editing with a live preview. "Trust words you've typed − N + times": your words aren't auto-corrected or underlined. "Suggest learned & personal words" off no longer deletes learned words. Remove clears every capitalisation, a removed word typed again comes back from the bottom. Undo / redo work after tapping the cursor elsewhere. Review fixes from the 21-item review; navigation-bar colour and five search-only rows removed. |
| 0.3.004 | 3004 | Learning, per script | Everything in 0.3.003 (never uploaded on its own), plus: learned words and the blacklist kept per script, not per language (migrated on first start, old files kept aside), "Share learned & blacklisted words across keyboards" (Advanced, on); the "Learned & blacklisted words" screen (Learned / Blacklisted per script); Remove also deletes from Android's personal dictionary, with strikes (the more often removed, the harder it comes back); learned words fade when unused (3-month steps to 12.5 %, 50 % floor for words used 5+ times) and are never deleted; corrections count (hand-corrected +4, reverted auto-correction nets 0, undo / redo reverse / reapply exactly); "Log corrections"; words with a mistapped digit ("Ha0py", "3stimate") suggested right; undo after a tap fixed; store caps 25k words / 80k pairs. |
| 0.3.005 | 3005 | Spaces, addresses, learning rules | "Autospace after punctuation" now covers the full stop too: the space appears at once ("Sender. First. Last") and is taken back with the keyboard's own capitals when the text turns out to be an address: at its "@" (backspace on the "@" puts them back) or when a web ending that isn't a word is finished ("google. Com" + space → "google.com "); no space after a single letter (e.g., U.S.), between digits (3.14, v2.0) or after "www."; with URL detection off, none after an "@" for up to 5 full stops. Learning by space is tight: letters with ' - . between them (don't, well-known, hello.world, google.com) and email addresses; anything else typed without a space (digits, "+", ":" …, or the piece after one) is learned only by a tap in the strip, which now also offers everything typed since the last space ("sender+tag@gmail.com"). Unicode 17 emoji recognised (backspace deletes them whole); the unit tests are green (HeliBoard's two known failures skipped as in their CI). |
| 0.3.006 | 3006 | About moved, Advanced learning and swiping | Advanced is now "App settings", with About as its last row (no longer on the main screen). New "Advanced learning and swiping" (advanced only): the swipe tuning, its statistics and the swipe logs (moved from Swiping), "Trust words you've typed" (moved from Text correction) and "Log corrections"; these are the same for every keyboard (the keyboard in use gives its values once). |
| 0.3.007 | 3007 | Learned words in swipe, settings layout | Swiping knows your learned words again right after the keyboard restarts (they were read with a time limit that a bigger store missed), and learned words are saved a few seconds after each change, not only when you leave a text field. A word you correct by hand counts more than a pick from the strip. App settings is on the main screen in simple mode too: its own settings first, then "Separate settings per keyboard" with a checkbox per menu (each keyboard keeps that menu to itself) and its learned words, then About. New "Backspace long press delay" (advanced). Both long press delays sit below the keypress sounds. Long-press on a number key can open the settings, and the swipe trail can stay until the keyboard hides. "Swiping" is now "Swipe". "Number of suggestions" (Text correction) always shows how many the strip shows. "Advanced learning and swiping" is not shown for now; its settings keep working at their values. Clearer English wording in several places; app-wide key gaps 0.5 / 0.75 become 1 / 2 once. |
| 0.3.008 | 3008 | Production launch defaults | First production release. New installs get HeliBoard's long-press popups (main accents, no symbol map; the richer arrangement with the symbol map is the "Curmudgeon" preset under Layout & Typing → Popups), a suggestion strip in the swipe trail's colour unless you pick one in Appearance → Fonts, and long-press on ?123 opening the settings. Settings you never changed follow the new defaults: a setting is kept only when you picked it, so backups and saved themes and Layouts no longer write down the ones at their default (one-time cleanup of those written before). Popup presets: HeliBoard (default), HeliBoard extra popups (all accents), Curmudgeon. Press and hold on the period key types an apostrophe. Fixes: learned words take the learned-word boost as it is set now; the keyboard opens without building the swipe word list on the main thread; a word typed right after deleting a swipe counts extra only if it's a known word typed as is; long-press ?123 to Settings or Emoji comes back on the letters; the swipe results no longer count a swipe twice; saving a theme or a backup keeps the suggestion colour following the trail. |
